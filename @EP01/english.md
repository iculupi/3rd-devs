## YouTube Description
Join Holy Luca on a journey to uncover the secrets of a new martial tradition in Tibia! When the High Scryer Fernlor detects a magical anomaly on an uncharted island, our adventure begins. We explore the demon-infested Azzilon Castle, battle ancient inferniarchs, and meet the mysterious Lai who introduces us to the legacy of the Merudri monks.

Follow along as we:
- Investigate Fernlor's strange anomaly
- Discover the ruins of Azzilon Castle
- Learn about the ancient plant people who once inhabited it
- Encounter various types of inferniarchs
- Free the prisoner Ortelio
- Disrupt the demon prince Arbaziloth's operations
- Witness a betrayal by the Ominous Stranger
- Confront Arbaziloth in an epic battle

This adventure marks the beginning of our discovery of the monk tradition and the ancient Merudri civilization whose martial arts prowess and spiritual enlightenment will change the balance of power in Tibia forever.

# The Merudri

## Overview
The Merudri are an ancient, seclusive people who developed powerful martial arts skills and mystical abilities. Nearly pushed to extinction, they unified in their will to survive and preserve their culture. After a long period of isolation, they have begun to emerge from hiding to seek allies and pass their unique abilities to a new generation.

## Physical Characteristics
- Dark skin tone that contrasts with glowing symbols on their skin
- Glowing eyes that manifest their magical and spiritual strength
- Hair length indicates rank within their society
- Athletic, muscular build emphasizing their physical prowess

## Culture & Society

### Core Principles
The Merudri philosophy is built on three fundamental elements:
1. Harmony
2. Enlightenment
3. Power

These elements are represented symbolically by a triangle within a circle, representing the principle of totality.

### Architecture & Design
- Circular motifs representing totality
- Triangular elements symbolizing the three core principles
- Blend of Far Eastern Asian influences (Shaolin, Tibetan, Hindu) with unique Tibian elements
- Temple features:
  - Rounded rooftops
  - Earthy brown-orange color palette
  - Ornate columns and detailed wall sections
  - Specialized shrines and light sources
  - Training circles
  - Writing desks

### Sanctuary
The "Blue Valley" serves as their secluded exile and sanctuary, carefully designed to reflect their cultural values and martial traditions.

## History

### Near Extinction
The Merudri faced a series of events that almost led to their complete destruction. This crisis forced them into hiding and shaped their current cautious approach to revealing their culture.

### Modern Era
The Merudri have recently decided to emerge from seclusion, driven by:
- Need to preserve their cultural heritage
- Desire to pass on their knowledge to worthy students
- Recognition of growing threats requiring allies
- Mission to train a new generation of monks

## Legacy
The Merudri's influence extends beyond their martial arts:
- Ancient combat techniques
- Spiritual practices
- Philosophical teachings
- Architectural design
- Symbolic language and glyphs
- Magical understanding

## Artifacts & Symbols

### The Trinity Mosaic
A sacred representation found in Merudri temples showing:
- Central triangle within a circle
- Three figures representing cosmic forces:
  1. The Eye and Scroll (Uman-Zathroth)
  2. The Sun (Fafnar)
  3. The Moon (Suon)
- Red circular backgrounds symbolizing power
- Geometric patterns representing order and harmony

### Religious Significance
The Merudri's understanding of the cosmic forces shaped their martial arts:
- Balance between creative and destructive forces (Uman-Zathroth)
- Determination and willpower (Fafnar)
- Patience and wisdom (Suon)

### Symbolic Elements
- Triangle: Unity of the three cosmic forces
- Circle: Totality and completion
- Eye: Divine sight and wisdom
- Scroll: Knowledge and power
- Red circles: Divine energy and enlightenment

### Training weapons (nunchakus, sai forks, claw gauntlets, katars, battle staffs)
- Temple mosaics depicting their history
- Ceremonial items
- Glowing tattoos and glyphs
- Traditional writing desks and training circles

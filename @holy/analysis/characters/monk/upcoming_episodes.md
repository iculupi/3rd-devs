### Opening (45 seconds)
- Holy Luca introduces the focus on monk combat abilities and magic
- Frame the episode as a practical guide to the new vocation

### Fundamental Philosophy (60 seconds)
- Explanation of the Harmony system as core mechanic
- How it differs from traditional Tibia combat and magic
- Visual demonstration of Harmony points being generated and spent

### Builder Spells (90 seconds)
- Explanation of builder category and how these generate Harmony
- Showcase of key builder spells:
  - Flurry of Blows (basic AoE attack)
  - Greater Flurry of Blows (enhanced version)
  - Chained Penance (target + chain effect)

  - Virtue of Sustain (defensive focus)

- How virtues modify abilities and provide passive bonuses
- When to use each virtue based on team composition and situation

### Serene State (60 seconds)
- Explanation of the Serene mechanic
- Conditions required to enter Serene state
- Benefits during Serene state
- Strategic timing for maximum effectiveness
- Focus Serenity spell and its benefits

### Support Abilities (75 seconds)
- Overview of monk utility spells:
  - Mentor Other (buffs based on target vocation)
  - Cleansing Wave (condition removal)
  - Inner Peace (mana recovery)
  - Shifting Mirage (movement/positioning)

### Advanced Techniques (90 seconds)
- High-level gameplay demonstrations:
  - Harmony point management
  - Virtue switching
  - Positioning for maximum AoE effectiveness
  - Damage vs. support role balance
  - Synergies with other vocations

### Equipment Considerations (60 seconds)
- How weapons affect monk abilities
  - Physical vs. elemental weapons
  - Critical hit weapons
  - Imbuing considerations
- Armor choices and their effects on monk playstyle
- New equipment attributes (mantra, elemental bond)

### Combat Rotations (75 seconds)
- Example rotations for different scenarios:
  - Solo hunting
  - Team support
  - Boss fighting
  - PvP considerations

### Closing (45 seconds)
- Holy Luca summarizes the monk's unique approach to combat
- Teaser for final episode on monk integration with existing game systems

# A Vision of the Past

## Basic Information
**Role**: Ethereal memory of the original inhabitants of Azzilon  
**Nature**: Spectral entity, final remnant of the plant-like race  
**Location**: Accessible through a special vision granted by Lai  
**Physical Description**: Not explicitly described physically, but represents the consciousness of the plant-like race that built and defended Azzilon

## Background
A Vision of the Past is not an individual in the conventional sense, but rather the collective memory of a once-proud race of plant-like beings who served as champions of the gods of light and good. These beings built the fortress of Azzilon as a bastion against evil forces but were ultimately overwhelmed by the inferniarchs in a devastating siege.

This spectral entity represents the final echo of a civilization that has been completely wiped out, with its memory somehow preserved within the walls of Azzilon Castle. The being describes itself as "what is no longer alive. A mere memory stored in the walls of our fallen fortress."

## Role in the Story
The Vision serves as the primary source of historical information about Azzilon Castle and the ancient war between the gods of good and evil. Through conversation with this entity, players learn about:

1. The origin and purpose of Azzilon fortress
2. The nature of the plant people who built it
3. The catastrophic siege by inferniarchs that led to their downfall
4. The tragic miscalculation regarding time that doomed their defense plans
5. The ultimate weapon that never had a chance to develop

This historical context deepens the player's understanding of the current situation in Azzilon and foreshadows the importance of time and patience in the monk philosophy.

## Personality
The Vision displays a melancholic, reflective personality colored by the tragedy it has witnessed. Its manner of speaking is poetic and somewhat formal, befitting a being that has existed for eons. Key traits include:

- **Mournful**: Frequently references failure and lost opportunities
- **Philosophical**: Draws broader lessons from specific events
- **Dignified**: Despite recounting a devastating defeat, maintains a sense of pride in what they once were
- **Warning**: Eager to ensure others learn from their mistakes, particularly regarding the value of time

## Complete Dialogue Collection

### Introduction
"Greetings visitor."

"The place you are visiting, is nothing but an echo of the past."

"This place and I are just visions of a long gone area."

"I am what is no longer alive. A mere memory stored in the walls of our fallen fortress."

"The last memory of a once proud race, wiped out in their war against the demons."

### About Their Race
"Once we were numerous. We seeded the world, that the gods entrusted us to protect. We became their champions and wardens in their wars against evil."

"We planted the seed for the ultimate weapon, safely hidden in the deepest depths of the high seas. If it had grown, it would have turned the tide of battle. But we underestimated time."

### About The War
"We fought in the name of the gods. We fought valiantly. We fought gloriously. Yet ultimately we failed."

"We were great soldiers. Many battles were won by our wooden legions by physical prowess, magical insight, and the blessings of the gods. Yet we were not prepared for the fury of the inferniarchs. The inferniarchs managed to burn their hellish portals into weak pockets of the rock and from there wormed their way into the bowels of our fortress while we were fighting off their siege engines. Even then we might have stood a chance, but we were not prepared for Rotrender."

"We prepared for the great war to come. We planted the seeds for our future armies all over the world. A legion to behold would have risen, with the passing of the seasons. But before the seeds could flourish, the sheer number of demons and their ferocity overwhelmed us."

"The legions were prepared to bloom, but without us to teach the next generation, our once proud race will fall into barbarism and disarray. Or already has, as we are only a faint memory without substance or identity now."

### About Their Failure
"The fortress of Azzilon has fallen. Our race has perished. All our preparations were for naught. Even our ultimate weapon wasn't prepared to save us."

"We thought in the dimensions of trees, time meant little to us. It was an ally at best. But time turned out to become our worst enemy."

"Time had run out for us. We lost the next generations that we planted, and our folly doomed our civilization. Our best weapon was not even blossoming when the demons came. This doomed us all."

"This echo is our last legacy, the legacy of a lost race. Let our fate be a reminder for others not to underestimate time. The things you seem to have in abundance may turn on you at some point. Never forget, that time is precious, even when you seem to have it in abundance."

### About Rotrender
"He was a massive inferniarch, the wildest and most despicable of them all. Where he walked, the ground was defiled and rot nested. His touch withered our bark and caused us to crumble. He became the scourge of Azzilon. Our doom. In the end, we lured him into a cave where some of our finest had merged with stone and earth. Their roots collapsed the cave over the fiend, crushing his poisonous body under masses of rock, ending its existence. But the damage had been done, our defences breached. The hordes of the inferniarchs were everywhere. We did not go out without a glorious battle, taking inferniarchs with us in droves. In the end they brought us down though. One by one the defenders fell and at some point even the victorious cackling of the remaining inferniarchs ceased and silence fell over Azzilon, to be forgotten by the world we fought for."

## Thematic Significance
The Vision of the Past serves several important thematic functions in the monk class storyline:

1. **Time and Patience**: The plant people's fatal error was underestimating how quickly their enemy would strike, thinking "in the dimensions of trees" rather than in mortal timescales. This foreshadows the monk philosophy's emphasis on balance and timing.

2. **Cycles of History**: The ancient conflict between good and evil that played out at Azzilon is now repeating with new actors—Lai and the player versus Arbaziloth and the renegade Merudri.

3. **Legacy and Memory**: Despite the complete destruction of their civilization, something of the plant people survives in memory, suggesting that wisdom and knowledge can persist even when physical forms are destroyed.

4. **Sacrifice**: The plant people's valiant last stand, particularly against Rotrender, demonstrates the noble sacrifice that sometimes must be made in the fight against evil.

## Connections to Monk Philosophy
While not explicitly linked to the Merudri or monk class, several aspects of the Vision's tale resonate with monk philosophy:

- The importance of harmony with nature (the plant people were literally one with nature)
- The long view of time and patience (though in this case, it proved their downfall)
- The concept of sacrifice for greater good
- The cycle of growth, death, and rebirth

## Additional Notes
- The plant people had a unique relationship with time, viewing it through the lens of trees rather than mortal lifespans
- They had technology or magic advanced enough to create an "ultimate weapon" hidden in the depths of the seas
- Their method of reproduction involved planting seeds that would grow into new generations
- Some members of their race could merge with stone and earth, suggesting powerful nature magic
- Their legacy includes both Azzilon itself and the warning about time's precious nature

# The History of Azzilon Castle

## Origins
Azzilon was once a magnificent bastion dedicated to the gods of light and good, constructed in an age before the rise of humanity. The fortress was built and inhabited by a race of plant-like beings who served as champions of the gods in the ancient war against evil. These plant people were highly advanced, utilizing both martial prowess and magical insight in their battles.

## Architecture
The castle features a distinctive stone architecture with fortified walls and multiple towers positioned at the cardinal points (northwest, northeast, southeast, and southwest). Each tower housed powerful crystals that, when activated together, created a protective ward around the fortress. At the center of the castle stood a colossal tree, now burned and withered, which likely served as both a spiritual focus and a source of power for its plant-like inhabitants.

## The Great Siege
Before Azzilon could be fully staffed and prepared, it came under siege by the forces of evil, primarily a breed of demon-like beings known as inferniarchs. These ancient demons were the ancestors of the "modern" demons known today, but with a crucial difference: their magical energy was permanently fused into their physical forms, unable to return to the magic web upon death.

The siege was devastating. While the defenders fought valiantly and slew countless inferniarchs, they were ultimately overwhelmed. The turning point came when a particularly vicious inferniarch known as Rotrender breached the defenses. This monstrous being exuded decay and rot, causing the plant defenders to wither and crumble at its touch.

In a last act of desperation, the defenders lured Rotrender into a cave where some of their finest warriors had merged with stone and earth. They collapsed the cave, crushing Rotrender beneath tons of rock. However, by then, the damage was done - the defenses had been breached, and the inferniarch hordes poured in from all sides.

## The Fall
One by one, the defenders fell. They fought gloriously to the end, taking droves of inferniarchs with them, but they could not stem the tide. Eventually, the last defender fell, and silence descended upon Azzilon. The fortress was left abandoned, littered with the undecaying corpses of inferniarchs that had been slain in the battle.

The plant people had planned for reinforcements - seeds had been planted across the world that would have grown into a mighty legion given time. They had even created an "ultimate weapon" hidden in the depths of the high seas. However, they had underestimated how quickly the enemy would strike. In the words of the Vision of the Past: "We thought in the dimensions of trees, time meant little to us. It was an ally at best. But time turned out to become our worst enemy."

## Recent Events
For aeons, Azzilon lay forgotten, a silent monument to a fallen civilization. Recently, however, a demon prince named Arbaziloth discovered the fortress and recognized the potential power that remained locked in the ancient inferniarch corpses. He established his operations there, bringing demons and enslaved trolls to extract the ancient magical energy.

The situation was further complicated when a renegade from the Merudri people (an ancient monk race) allied with Arbaziloth. This renegade used his knowledge to develop a method of harvesting the magical energy from the inferniarch corpses - energy that had been bound to their forms for eternity. 

This extraction had an unforeseen consequence: the emptied inferniarch corpses rose as a new form of undead, driven by an insatiable hunger for what they had lost. These reanimated inferniarchs now roam the castle and its catacombs, presenting a threat to all who enter.

## Current State
Currently, Azzilon serves as the base of operations for Arbaziloth and his demonic forces. The castle is occupied by various types of inferniarchs, both living demons brought by Arbaziloth and the reanimated corpses of ancient ones. The catacombs beneath the castle contain slave quarters where trolls are forced to work for the demons, as well as chambers where the harvested energy is processed and refined.

The center of the castle features a flying carpet above the burned-out remains of the colossal tree, where Lai, a Merudri monk, has established herself to monitor and counter the demonic operations. The entire area is affected by a toxic, demonic mist that causes continuous damage to intruders, though this can be mitigated by collecting demonic core essences.

The fortress now stands as a critical battleground where the future of the Monk class is being determined, as players must help Lai defeat Arbaziloth and perhaps uncover the deeper mysteries of the Merudri people and their connection to this ancient place.

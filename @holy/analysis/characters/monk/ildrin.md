# Ildrin

## Basic Information
**Role**: Adventurer who encountered the Renegade (Husk) in Azzilon's catacombs  
**Location**: Mentioned only in the teaser about the Renegade  
**Current Status**: Presumably deceased, shot by Husk  

## Background
Ildrin appears only in one of the teasers related to the monk class introduction, specifically in a narrative that focuses on the Renegade Merudri known as Husk. From this limited information, we can determine that Ildrin was an adventurer exploring the cursed catacombs of Azzilon, fighting against the demonic inferniarch hordes.

The teaser suggests that Ildrin had been battling demons for days, with dwindling supplies and faded imbuements on his equipment. This indicates he was a seasoned adventurer accustomed to extended expeditions into dangerous territory.

Ildrin appears to have been traveling or fighting alongside Husk, though their relationship is unclear. He demonstrates curiosity about Husk's unusual resilience and power, particularly the strange bow that Husk carries.

## From the Teaser
The only information about Ildrin comes from this narrative passage:

"Deep in the cursed catacombs of Azzilon, Ildrin's resolve wavers as his eyes fall on yet another demonic symbol scorched into the ground. His blade has tasted demon blood for days on end, yet the infernal hordes persist. His supplies dwindle, and the imbuements he relied upon have long faded, leaving him vulnerable and weak.

But one figure remains undeterred: Husk. Clad in rotting carcasses he pulled from a crevice of corpses, Husk moves with unrelenting strength, each strike of his bow biting critically deep into demonic flesh. 'How does he endure?' Ildrin wonders.

Husk's bow glows faintly, a soft light emanating from its strings as it unleashes another rapid barrage with effortless grace. He clutches it close to his chest when he sleeps, keeping its secrets hidden. In one swift motion, Husk destroys the last of the inferniarchs with deadly precision. Their ancient, twisted forms collapse at his feet and Ildrin breathes a sigh of relief.

But all of a sudden, Husk catches sight of the demonic symbol. He lunges forward, shoving Ildrin violently away from it: 'Move!' His voice thunders with urgency.

As they tumble to the ground, something dark slips from Husk's pockets, landing on the cold stone: a shriveled piece of skin, brimming with unnatural energy. It feels similar to Husk's bow, but hums a slightly different, yet equally sinister tune.

Ildrin reaches out to grab it. 'Don't touch it!' Husk's voice breaks through, sharp as steel. 'It shall seal the fate of those who stand against me.' Ildrin's hand hovers, trembling, before he cautiously pulls it back. 'Is that what powers your bow?' his voice is filled with awe.

Husk's eyes flare red in response, both as a warning and a threat. 'Do NOT touch it. I will bring it to the Forgemaster. As I did with the other commander's hide. It is MINE.'

The air between them thickens. Husk draws his bow with deliberate slowness and nocks an arrow, his gaze locked on Ildrin. 'The Forgemaster?' Ildrin's voice falters as the arrowhead aligns with his heart. Husk's smile is cold as ice: 'He has already forged your doom.'

With a snap, the arrow strikes.

A final judgment."

## Character Traits
From this brief appearance, we can infer several traits about Ildrin:

- **Experienced Fighter**: Has been battling demons for days
- **Resourceful**: Survived in the catacombs despite dwindling supplies
- **Observant**: Notices details about Husk's bow and behavior
- **Curious**: Questions Husk's endurance and the source of his power
- **Somewhat Naive**: Reaches for the strange item despite obvious danger signs
- **Cautious**: Ultimately pulls his hand back rather than touching the item

## Role in the Story
Ildrin serves several narrative functions in the monk class storyline:

1. **Witness**: Provides an outside perspective on the Renegade's unusual abilities and items
2. **Victim**: Demonstrates the ruthlessness of the Renegade toward those who discover his secrets
3. **Contrast**: His normal human limitations (fading strength, dwindling supplies) highlight the Renegade's unnatural endurance
4. **Plot Device**: His curiosity leads to the revelation about the Forgemaster and the source of the Renegade's power

## Significance
Though Ildrin appears to be a minor character who meets a swift end, his brief appearance reveals critical information about the Renegade and the nature of his corruption. Through Ildrin's observations and questions, we learn:

1. The Renegade possesses a bow with unusual magical properties
2. He collects "commander's hide" and other items with "unnatural energy"
3. He has a relationship with someone called "the Forgemaster" who creates weapons
4. He is willing to kill without hesitation to protect his secrets

Ildrin's apparent death at the Renegade's hands establishes the high stakes of the conflict and the dangerous nature of the fallen Merudri that Lai is tracking.

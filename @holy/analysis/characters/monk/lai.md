# Lai

## Basic Information
**Race**: Merudri (ancient race of monk warriors)  
**Gender**: Female  
**Location**: Top of Azzilon Castle, standing on a flying carpet above a burned tree  
**Physical Description**: Tall, blonde woman with serene composure and elegant bearing  

## Background
Lai is a traveling monk from the Blue Valley, the sacred homeland of the Merudri people. She was dispatched on a mission to track down a renegade of her people who has allied with demons. This renegade was once one of the greatest minds among the Merudri, but he turned to forbidden practices and ultimately allied himself with the demon prince Arbaziloth, lending him power, special talents, and knowledge.

Lai's presence at Azzilon Castle represents a turning point in Merudri history, as her interaction with outsiders marks a shift away from the traditionally isolationist policies of her people. As she mentions, "Not long ago, my predecessors would have brought the fiery rage of the Claw upon you...! Well, times are changing and so do we."

## Role in the Story
Lai serves as the primary quest-giver for the "No Rest for the Wicked" quest line that introduces players to the monk class. She guides adventurers through the process of defeating Arbaziloth and helps them learn about both the immediate threat and the deeper history of the Merudri people.

After Arbaziloth's defeat, she also introduces the "Mystery of the Valley" quest, which leads to the reconstruction of the Guardian Statue and reveals more about Merudri culture and the Blue Valley.

Lai represents the bridge between the secretive Merudri civilization and the outside world, embodying a progressive faction among her people who believe the time has come to share their knowledge and traditions with others.

## Personality
Lai is wise, composed, and philosophical. She speaks in formal language often relating to concepts of harmony and balance. Despite the dangerous environment of Azzilon Castle, she maintains a sense of purposeful tranquility. She is bound by certain oaths and obligations that limit what she can reveal about her people, though she expresses that this secrecy "harrows her deeply."

She shows respect for tradition while also embracing necessary change, as evidenced by her willingness to work with outsiders when previous generations of her people would have refused such contact.

Those ancient demons though were fused with their magic which is trapped in their dead bodies for all eternity. That is until the renegade made his alliance with the demon prince. I don't know how the demons repaid his services but he used his powers to free the magic of the dead demons, so the demon prince could use it to strengthen himself and his legions. ### Quest Dialogue - Mystery of the Valley

#### About the Statue and Blue Valley
"Who told you about this? Knowledge of the valley was secret and outside interference forbidden for centuries. Not long ago, my predecessors would have brought the fiery rage of the Claw upon you...! Well, times are changing and so do we. The blue valley is the sacred home and remnant of our civilisation, the ancient Merudri, my kin. In any case, you're probably not even the first outsider to learn about it. Legend has it among our kin that a stranger once glimpsed upon the valley when it was still forbidden for outsiders to walk Merudri soil. But I should not talk about this."

#### The Legend of the Trespasser
"Well, there's no harm in telling it to someone like you, I assume. So, let me tell you a story about a trespasser, a wayfaring adventurer, not unlike you. The wayfarer witnessed a pilgrimaging Merudri travelling back to the valley, using one of our guardian statues. Three dragons holding a powerful crystal that mingles with time and space. When he approached the crystal, another pilgrim appeared and destroyed the crystal with a mighty blow, turning to the trespasser as he picks up a piece of debris from the smashed crystal. Thus, he escaped the all-seeing eyes of the valley guards. It is said that the stranger carved a wooden miniature from it, carefully modelling every minute detail and even placing a crystal fragment on top of it. We do not know if this nameless wanderer ever tried to return to the valley but the power in the crystal fragment of the statue would not suffice to allow travel between any point in this world and the valley. However, there was still a flicker of ancient power left in the stolen fragment. Putting the miniature together, something must have struck a terrible fear in the heart of the interloper. Some say the face of a Dhar-Enpa has appeared in a facet of the crystal, others say he was struck by lightning the very moment the statue was put together. Terrified, he threw the statue away, breaking it in the process. To atone for his transgressions and not to anger the Merudri any further, he build three shrines across the world to house its fragments and honour the Merudri. The guardians followed his every step since the theft of the fragment. A kind Enpa decided to leave the stranger alone and kept the whereabouts of these shrines hidden as any interference with our culture outside of the valley remained prohibited. Only Enpa Udra knows the exact locations of the shrines. They are of no worth for us but we respect the remorse of the trespasser towards our people in hindsight. Why are you looking at me like that? As I said, the statue doesn't have any actual purpose. Do you really want to find it?"

#### First Fragment
"If you want to search for them yourself, I will have to channel Dhar-Enpa Udra, who has left his physical form a long time ago. This will take a few days, however. The best way to start looking while I conduct the channelling would be to search the cold places of this world as one of the fragments is definitely hidden in the eternal ice."

"Enpa Udra tries to show me location... I cannot see clearly yet. It's all white and chaotic. The first shrine seems hidden in ice and snow, not far from a large human settlement."

#### Second Fragment
"Enpa Udra showed me the location. I can clearly see the first shrine now, it's on a small cliff near the city of Svargrond. The second one however... There is heat shimmering, insects buzzing and a lot of green. Bamboo trees and grass. A different shrine sits right next to it."

#### Third Fragment
"Enpa Udra showed me the location. I can see two of the shrines very clearly now, the first is on a small cliff near the city of Svargrond, the second one on a small plateau in the land Zao. The third one is somewhat clear but still too hazy to make it out exactly. It's a dry area and there are large rocks and cliffs. A large river flows through the land and saturates the barren landscape. Someone seems to walk around not far from it."

#### Before Assembling the Statue
"You found all three missing pieces, congratulations! To combine them, you'll need a few more crystal fragments from the original statue. Enpa Udra showed me the location where the statue once stood: atop a rock on the foot of the Kha'zeel mountains. I saw cracks in the stone there, so bring a shovel or pick. If you can still find some of the fragments of the shattered crystal, you may be able to reconstruct the statue using them to mend the three pieces and the crystal."

#### After Assembling the Statue
"So, you reconstructed the guardian statue. You know, it was scattered and hidden for a reason back then. Oh well, Dahr-Enpa Udra doesn't seem to mind. Maybe it is time to finally invite outsiders into the valley and Udra has seen its future. But that's certainly not for you or me to decide."

## Relationships
- **The Renegade (Husk)**: A former member of her people whom she was sent to track down. She views his betrayal with sorrow and determination to stop him.
- **Arbaziloth**: The demon prince she seeks to defeat with the player's help. She recognizes him as a significant threat.
- **Ortelio**: An ally in the fight against the demons, whom she helps rescue and then works with.
- **Enpa Udra**: A spiritual guide of her people who has transcended physical form and helps locate the statue fragments.
- **Holy Luca**: While not directly interacting with him in the quests, he serves as the narrator introducing her story to the players.

## Significance
Lai represents the first direct contact players have with the monk class and the Merudri culture. Her presence in Azzilon marks a turning point not only in the game's lore but also in the history of her people, as they begin to open up to outsiders after centuries of isolation.

She embodies the core philosophies of the monk class: harmony, balance, and disciplined power. Her role as both a mentor and a quest-giver establishes the foundation for players' understanding of monk abilities and Merudri culture.

## Additional Notes
- Lai's flying carpet above the burned tree symbolizes her elevated status above the corruption and chaos of Azzilon.
- She has the ability to "harmonize" players with the essence of places, allowing them to perceive visions of the past.
- She can channel the wisdom of departed Enpas, particularly Dhar-Enpa Udra.
- Though she cannot reveal everything about her people due to oaths and obligations, she clearly desires greater openness and exchange with the outside world.

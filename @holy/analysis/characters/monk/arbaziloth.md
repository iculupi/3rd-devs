# Arbaziloth

## Basic Information
**Role**: Demon Prince, main antagonist of the monk class introduction storyline  
**Nature**: Powerful demon, ambitious even among his kind  
**Location**: Deepest level of Azzilon Castle catacombs  
**Physical Description**: Not explicitly described in detail, but as a Demon Prince, likely imposing and fearsome in appearance. During the boss fight, he radiates malevolent power enhanced by the stolen energy of ancient inferniarchs.

## Background
Arbaziloth is an ambitious demon prince who discovered the ancient fortress of Azzilon and recognized the potential power locked within the undecaying corpses of the inferniarchs—demon ancestors whose magical energy remained bound to their physical forms even after death. 

He established his base of operations at Azzilon, bringing demons and enslaved trolls to extract and process this ancient magical energy. His plans were significantly advanced when he formed an alliance with a renegade from the Merudri people (referred to as "Husk" in some accounts). This renegade used his twisted knowledge to develop a method of harvesting the magical energy from the inferniarch corpses, energy that had previously been thought bound to their forms for eternity.

This extraction had an unforeseen consequence: the emptied inferniarch corpses rose as a new form of undead, driven by an insatiable hunger for what they had lost. These reanimated inferniarchs now roam the castle alongside Arbaziloth's living demonic forces.

## Role in the Story
Arbaziloth serves as the primary antagonist and final boss of the "No Rest for the Wicked" quest line that introduces the monk class. His activities at Azzilon—specifically his alliance with the renegade Merudri and their extraction of ancient magical energy—represent the threat that brings Lai to the fortress and ultimately leads to the player's involvement.

Throughout the quest, players work to disrupt Arbaziloth's operations by freeing troll slaves, interrupting energy extraction, and finally confronting him directly in his lair. His defeat marks the completion of the first quest line, though the escape of his Merudri ally suggests that the larger threat has not been fully eliminated.

## Personality
Arbaziloth displays classic demonic traits of arrogance, cruelty, and lust for power. His dialogue during the final confrontation reveals his inflated self-image and contempt for those who challenge him. At the same time, his alliance with the renegade Merudri suggests a certain pragmatism and willingness to work with others when it serves his purposes.

Key traits include:
- **Arrogant**: Believes himself superior to others, especially humans
- **Ambitious**: Seeks to gather power beyond what other demons possess
- **Calculating**: Forms strategic alliances to achieve his goals
- **Cruel**: Enslaves trolls and shows no mercy to opponents

## Operations in Azzilon

Based on the quest descriptions and dialogue from both Lai and Ortelio, Arbaziloth's operations in Azzilon include:

1. **Slave Labor**: Using enslaved trolls as a workforce, keeping them weak and controlled through minimal feeding
2. **Energy Extraction**: Harvesting magical energy from ancient inferniarch corpses
3. **Energy Processing**: Refining and cooling stolen energy in specialized vats
4. **Powered Imps**: Using charged imps to collect and process the stolen energy
5. **Personal Enhancement**: Has four powerful energy spheres implanted in his body for protection and power
6. **Warding Systems**: Creating magical wards to protect his lair
7. **Minion Control**: Commanding various types of inferniarchs and other demons

## The Final Battle

The confrontation with Arbaziloth takes place in the lowest level of the catacombs, where he has established his throne. According to Ortelio, Arbaziloth has four energy spheres implanted in his body that grant him immense powers and protection. The battle involves multiple phases:

1. **Sphere Protection**: Each sphere activates when Arbaziloth takes significant damage, making him temporarily immune
2. **Sphere Removal**: Players must cause explosions to loosen the spheres
3. **Fishing Mechanic**: Using fishing rods to remove the loosened spheres
4. **The Forgemaster Choice**: Players must decide whether to kill the Forgemaster (easier fight but no forge access) or let him escape to the forge (harder fight but forge access)

## Relationships
- **The Renegade Merudri (Husk)**: A crucial ally who provided the knowledge needed to extract energy from inferniarch corpses
- **The Forgemaster**: Appears to be connected to Arbaziloth's operations, providing access to special weapons after his defeat
- **Inferniarchs**: Commands both living demons brought to Azzilon and the reanimated corpses of ancient ones
- **Troll Slaves**: Enslaves and forces them to work for his operations
- **Lai**: His operations drew her to Azzilon to track down the renegade Merudri

## Significance
Arbaziloth represents the catalyst that brings the monk class into wider Tibian awareness. His alliance with the renegade Merudri forced Lai to leave the seclusion of the Blue Valley, beginning the process of the Merudri opening up to the outside world.

His defeat serves as both the conclusion of the introductory quest line and the beginning of a larger narrative about the monk class and its integration into Tibia. While Arbaziloth himself is vanquished, the escape of his Merudri ally suggests that the greater threat—the corruption of monk powers for evil purposes—remains at large.

## Additional Notes
- The violet color associated with the inferniarchs suggests a connection to Arbaziloth's particular brand of demonic magic
- His title "Demon Prince" indicates a position of authority among demonkind, though below the highest ranks
- His interest in ancient magical energy suggests he may have been seeking to elevate his status among demons
- The fact that he was defeated but his ally escaped creates a narrative hook for future monk-related content
- His operations represented a twofold threat: the immediate danger to the world and the corruption of Merudri traditions

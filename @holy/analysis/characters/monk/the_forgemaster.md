# The Forgemaster

## Basic Information
**Role**: Craftsman for demonic weapons, associate of the Renegade  
**Location**: Doomforge area after defeating Arbaziloth  
**Physical Description**: Not explicitly described in the available information  
**Known Creations**: Inferniarch Set weapons, items forged from "commander's hide"

## Background
The Forgemaster is a mysterious figure first mentioned in the teaser featuring the Renegade (Husk). He appears to be a craftsman specializing in the creation of magical weapons, particularly those with demonic origins or properties. In the teaser, Husk mentions bringing him materials (specifically a "commander's hide") to forge into weapons.

After defeating Arbaziloth, players who complete the boss fight 10 times in Hard difficulty gain access to a special room in what is called the Doomforge, where they can meet The Forgemaster and obtain the base version of any Inferniarch Set weapon of their choice.

While his full history and identity remain obscure, the Forgemaster's dialogue suggests he was formerly in service to the Renegade ("my former master"), indicating a change in allegiance or circumstances at some point.

## Role in the Story

The Forgemaster serves multiple functions in the monk class storyline:

1. **Reward Provider**: Offers powerful weapons to players who defeat Arbaziloth repeatedly
2. **Boss Lieutenant**: Fights alongside Arbaziloth in the final battle
3. **Strategic Choice**: Players must decide whether to kill him (preventing forge access) or let him escape (making the fight harder but gaining forge access)
4. **Mysterious Connection**: Links the current events to the larger narrative involving the Renegade
5. **Lore Expansion**: Hints at a deeper network beyond just Arbaziloth and the Renegade
6. **Future Plot Element**: His mention of "magical wards" and time-limited availability suggests ongoing importance

His presence adds complexity to the storyline, suggesting that the corruption of Merudri knowledge extends beyond just the Renegade himself to a network of associates with specialized skills.

## Personality
From his limited dialogue, The Forgemaster appears to be:

- **Cryptic**: Speaks in warnings and partial information
- **Knowledgeable**: Aware of ancient magical wards and their properties
- **Cautious**: Warns players about limitations of his offerings
- **Craftsmanlike**: Takes pride in his "early prototypes" despite their limitations

## Relationships
- **The Renegade (Husk)**: Former master, mentioned with a somewhat distanced tone suggesting their relationship has changed
- **Arbaziloth**: Connection unclear, but he has access to "Arbaziloth's private weapon chamber"
- **Player**: Provider of rewards, seemingly neutral in current interactions

## The Doomforge and Inferniarch Weapons
The "Doomforge" appears to be a special area accessible only to players who have proven themselves by defeating Arbaziloth multiple times on hard difficulty. Within this area, The Forgemaster offers access to powerful weapons from the Inferniarch Set.

These weapons are characterized by their connection to the ancient demons and may incorporate materials harvested from defeated creatures. This aligns with Husk's mention in the teaser of bringing "commander's hide" to The Forgemaster for crafting.

The suggestion that players can only choose one weapon "each thousand years" implies either:
1. An extremely long cooldown in game terms
2. A metaphorical expression emphasizing the rarity and value of the choice
3. A hint at magical time manipulation consistent with other elements of the monk storyline

## Symbolic Significance
The Forgemaster represents the material manifestation of corrupted knowledge—where the Renegade perverts spiritual and magical principles, The Forgemaster creates physical embodiments of that corruption in the form of weapons.

His title "Forgemaster" suggests not just craftsmanship but transformation—the reshaping of raw materials into new forms, paralleling how the Renegade has reshaped Merudri teachings into something unrecognizable and dangerous.

## Additional Notes
- The term "Doomforge" evokes both creation and destruction, fitting the dual nature of the monk storyline
- The limitation on weapon selection emphasizes the importance of choice and consequence in the monk's path
- His mention of "early prototypes" suggests ongoing development of these weapons, perhaps hinting at future content
- The relationship between The Forgemaster and the Renegade appears to have changed, but the nature of this change remains unclear
- His craftsmanship seems to incorporate materials from defeated enemies, suggesting a system of converting death into power

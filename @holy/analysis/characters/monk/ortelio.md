# Ortelio

## Basic Information
**Role**: Prisoner in Azzilon Castle / Explorer and informant  
**Gender**: Male  
**Location**: Initially found in a prison cell on the first lower level of Azzilon Castle, later assists with various tasks throughout the catacombs  
**Physical Description**: Not explicitly described, but appears to be human and has survived imprisonment by demons

## Background
Ortelio appears to be an explorer or researcher who was captured while investigating Azzilon Castle. Despite his imprisonment by the demons, he has managed to survive and gather valuable information about the demonic operations taking place in the castle and its catacombs. 

Unlike Fernlor who avoids danger, or Lai who has mystical abilities to protect herself, Ortelio represents the ordinary explorer who ventures into dangerous places and sometimes faces dire consequences. His survival in Azzilon's dangerous environment suggests he possesses considerable resourcefulness and determination.

During his captivity, he observed the demons' operations closely, learning about their use of troll slaves, the extraction of energy from inferniarch corpses, and the security systems they employed. This knowledge proves crucial for the disruption of Arbaziloth's plans.

## Role in the Story
Ortelio serves multiple functions in the monk class storyline:
1. **Rescue Target**: Initially, he is the "untainted soul in great despair" that Lai senses in the depths, whom the player must rescue
2. **Tactical Advisor**: After being freed, he provides crucial information about the demons' operations
3. **Guide**: He meets the player at various locations in the catacombs to help with disrupting demonic activities
4. **Problem Solver**: He figures out how to charge the player with stolen archaic energy to penetrate Arbaziloth's wards

His practical knowledge complements Lai's more mystical and historical insights, forming a balanced approach to the challenges in Azzilon Castle.

## Personality
Ortelio displays a straightforward, practical personality focused on survival and defeating the demons. His brief dialogue reveals him to be:
- **Direct and urgent**: His first words to the player are "Don't waste time. Get me out."
- **Observant**: He has gathered detailed information about the demons' operations despite being a prisoner
- **Resourceful**: He knows about the mummified demon fingers used to open his cell
- **Tactical**: He identifies key weaknesses in the demons' operations that can be exploited

He lacks the philosophical nature of Lai or the academic pride of Fernlor, instead focusing on immediate practical concerns.

### Mission: Feeding the Trolls
"Greetings. Let's not waste time with chit chat, but continue with the mission!"

"In this remote part of the complex, the demons house their enslaved trolls, that serve as the bulk of their workforce. We have to disrupt the demon's operations through freeing their troll slaves. Without their workers the demons would have to rely on themselves, severely hampering their operations and control here."

**About the Trolls:**
"The trolls are broken in body and spirit, lacking even the willpower to flee on their own. Yet if we could entice enough of them to flee, the horde mentality of the trolls will probably cause a mass breakout. Here we can turn the demons strategy against them! The demons feed the trolls with only the bare minimum, to keep them under control and break their will."

"They don't think about escaping while hungry. If you feed them though, they will regain some spirit for a while and run for safety. Sure, trolls being trolls, once hungry again, they will probably trod back into captivity, so you will have to keep them fed until they make it to the entrance."

"There should be some kind of food dispenser in the slave dorm that the trolls are conditioned to be afraid of, so they won't use it on their own. You though can use the dispenser and supply them with the food they covet. Make enough of them flee and get out before a sizeable troop of wardens shows up."

"I estimate that the escape of around 20 trolls should cause sufficient disruption, potentially even leading to a mass rout. Just feed the trolls and don't forget you are on a time budget. Every second wasted brings the demons closer to finding out what we are up to."

### Mission: Teach a Man to Fish
"We are now close to the cooling vats. Here the demons cool down spheres of their condensed stolen energy. As only demons can survive the energies ravaging the containment chambers, this place is one of the few where we can interfere with their tinkering."

"From here Arbaziloth himself is supplied with more and more energy. To breach the wards of his inner sanctum you have to infuse yourself with residual energy. You will need a fishing rod to get them out of the basins and charge yourself. Of course the imps working there won't be thrilled and attack you in the meantime. Any fished up sphere will immediately disperse and charge all of you with its energy. If you gained 25 charges you should be fine."

### Mission: With Friends Like These
**About the Mission:**
"Now you will challenge the vile Arbaziloth. He will be empowered and protected by spheres of their stolen energy. He has four of them implanted into his body. Granting him immense powers."

**About the Spheres:**
"The spheres will protect him to some extent. When he gets wounded for a certain amount a sphere will activate and block off all damage, technically rendering him immune to further harm. This is the time, at which you have to find a method to loosen the spheres in some way. Though it will probably need some massive explosion to do so and I have no idea how you could cause that."

"If you manage to do so though, you might be able to use your trusty fishing rods again, to steal one of the spheres from him. Then you should be able to wound him again, until the protection of the next sphere kicks in and you have to repeat the process. But all the while you will have to handle his forgemaster too."

**About Loosening the Spheres:**
"I guess you'd need something with a hook, that you can use on a distance. Something like an ordinary fishing rod should work."

**About the Forgemaster:**
"Arbaziloth's forgemaster is his right hand in his operations here. He is the overseer of his workforce and the administrator of the operation and his forge. He will probably join his master in the fight."

"Well, and here things get tricky. Of course you can kill the forgemaster outright, but then the forge will be kept locked, with no way for you to get in. If you let the forgemaster live though, and manage to hurt his master badly enough, he will surely retreat to the forge and thereby open it."

"BUT if he escapes to the forge, he will channel all of the forges energy into Arbaziloth, healing him and increasing his power significantly. So you will have to choose if you want an easier fight, but get no access to the forge, or you have a more difficult fight and get access the forge."

**About the Forge:**
"Only the forgemaster knows all of the details about that place. Apparently, that's where he gives his weapons a final touch by infusing them with the ancient demonic energy of this place. If you supply him with the right materials, of course..."

## Knowledge and Insights
Despite his imprisonment, Ortelio has gathered extensive intelligence about Azzilon, including:

1. **Cell Security**: Knowledge that mummified demon fingers are used to open certain magical locks
2. **Slave Operations**: Understanding of the troll slave workforce and how disrupting it would impact the demons
3. **Energy Extraction**: Information about how the demons process and refine energy from inferniarch corpses
4. **Warding Systems**: Knowledge of Arbaziloth's protective wards and how to penetrate them
5. **Castle Layout**: Familiarity with the layout of the catacombs and various chambers

## Significance
Ortelio represents the human element in the monk class storyline, providing a grounded perspective amidst the mystical Merudri and demonic entities. His practical knowledge serves as a counterpoint to Lai's more esoteric wisdom, offering players a more conventional approach to the challenges they face.

As a non-Merudri who has directly experienced the demons' cruelty, he also represents the immediate threat that Arbaziloth poses to the world at large, beyond the more specialized concerns of Lai's people.
